Micro Bargain ("we") runs a marketplace that connects customers with local shops in {{ city }}. This policy explains what personal data we collect and why, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Mobile number and name: to log you in with an OTP and to show shops who placed a booking.
  • Location: to show deals near you and, if you allow notifications, to alert you about new deals nearby. We store the last known location of your phone, not a history.
  • Delivery addresses: only if you choose home delivery.
  • Orders, reservations, reviews and support messages: to provide the service and resolve disputes.
  • Shop details and documents (merchants): to verify the shop before it can sell.
  • Device token: to send push notifications.

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details.

Who we share it with

  • The shop you book from sees your name and a masked phone number (the full number only for home delivery, after the shop accepts).
  • Service providers who run parts of the app for us: SMS (MSG91), push notifications (Google Firebase), payments (Razorpay), hosting.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active. Order and payment records are kept for 8 years as required by tax and accounting law.

Your rights

You can see and correct your profile in the app, turn notifications and location off in your phone settings, and delete your account from Profile → Delete account. Deleting removes your name, number, addresses and devices; past orders stay in anonymised form for accounting.

For any privacy request or grievance, write to our Grievance Officer at {{ contact_email }}. We reply within 7 days.
