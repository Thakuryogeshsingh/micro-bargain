Quick summary

  • Bad, stale, damaged, wrong or short items: report in the app within {{ complaint_hours }} hours with a photo.
  • The shop must answer within {{ reply_hours }} hours. If it does not, you get a full refund automatically.
  • Complaint refunds go to your Micro Bargain wallet instantly and can be used on your next order.
  • Cancelled online orders are refunded to your original payment method in 5 to 7 working days.

Quality complaints (all purchases)

  1. Open the order or booking in the app and tap "Report quality problem".
  2. Choose the problem (stale/rotten, damaged, expired, wrong item, less quantity, other), add a clear photo and a short note.
  3. The shop can refund you fully or partly, or disagree. If the shop disagrees, the Micro Bargain team reviews the photo and decides within 48 hours.
  4. Approved refunds are credited to your wallet at once. Only one complaint can be made per purchase.

This applies to cash and online payments alike, so you are protected even when you paid the shop directly.

Online payments

  • Shop cancels or does not respond: full refund, started automatically.
  • You cancel before the shop accepts: full refund, started automatically.
  • After the shop accepts: cancellation is not available in the app; contact the shop or raise a support ticket.
  • Wallet money used on a cancelled order goes back to your wallet straight away.
  • Bank refunds reach your account in 5 to 7 working days (bank timelines apply).

Wallet

Wallet money (cashback and refunds) can be used for up to {{ wallet_percent }}% of an order's items. It cannot be withdrawn to a bank account and has no cash value outside the app.
