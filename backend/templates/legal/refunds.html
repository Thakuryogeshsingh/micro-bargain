Online payments

  • Shop cancels or does not respond: full refund, started automatically.
  • You cancel before the shop accepts: full refund, started automatically.
  • After the shop accepts: cancellation is not available in the app; contact the shop or raise a support ticket.
  • Wrong, short or spoiled items: raise a support ticket within 24 hours with a photo. Approved refunds may be full or partial.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days (bank timelines apply).

Cash payments

For reservations and cash orders you pay the shop directly; any refund is given by the shop. Our support team will help resolve disputes.

Merchant plans and promotions

Prepaid and non-refundable, except when we reject or remove a promotion, in which case it is refunded in full.
